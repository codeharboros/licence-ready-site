What the check covers

The duties that actually get sponsors revoked.

Reporting — the clock matters

  • 10 working days: a worker doesn't start, 10+ days' unauthorised absence, salary below the CoS, role or work-location change, you stop sponsoring
  • 20 working days: change of address, name or structure; mergers; insolvency; convictions of key staff

Records & right-to-work

  • Right-to-work checks completed before employment starts, with follow-ups for time-limited visas
  • Appendix D records per worker: CoS, contact and address, role/salary/duties, NMC and other registrations, payroll
  • Role genuinely matches the CoS occupation code — a top revocation trigger
  • The expanded right-to-work checks in force since 1 October 2026 (now reaching contractors and gig workers)
